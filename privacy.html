Pin Wei Spend

Privacy notice

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Pin Wei Spend is an internal tool of Pin Wei (Công ty TNHH Pin Wei Việt Nam). Staff use it to submit expenses, have them approved, record payments and keep the petty cash book. Only people the company has added to its staff list can sign in. It is not offered to the public.

What the app uses

  • Your Google sign-in: your name and email address, to check that you are on the company's staff list. The app does not read your Gmail, Drive, contacts or anything else in your Google account.
  • What you enter: expenses and cash entries (amounts, descriptions, dates), the photos and PDF files you attach, and supplier or payee details, including bank account numbers.
  • A record of actions: who submitted, approved, rejected, paid or changed an expense, and when.
  • Staff details the company adds: your role and outlet and, where needed, a phone number and the bank account used to pay you back.
  • Telegram, if you connect it: the ID of your chat with the company's bot, so the app can send you alerts.

Where it is kept

Everything is stored in the company's own Google account (Google Sheets and Google Drive). Alerts are sent through Telegram and by email. Your browser keeps your sign-in for the session, an unsent draft of a new expense, and your light or dark theme choice.

Who can see it

Only signed-in staff, according to their role: you see your own expenses; approvers, the accountant and the owners see what they need to approve, pay and review. Full bank account numbers are shown only to the people who make the payments.

What the app does not do

It has no advertising and no analytics or tracking. The company does not sell this information or share it outside the company, except with the services named above that store it or deliver alerts.

How long it is kept

Expenses, payments and their history are part of the company's accounting records and are kept as long as the company needs them. To have your personal details corrected, or removed when you leave, ask the administrator.
